BH trainer

Recognise ZBLL from the BH pattern alone: the top face, the front-right corner and the two edges beside it. Everything else is grey, so you can't fall back on blocks. New to BH? Read How BH works.

Which BH pattern is this?

Press the letter on a button to answer. Drag the pattern buttons to put them in the order you like; the keys follow their places.

Front-right patterns

Each pattern is the front-right corner with the front and right edges. Pick the ones to train: you get every ZBLL, at every angle you chose, that shows one of them. Under each pattern: how many case angles have it, and how often you got it right.

The cube is set to the case in the picture, so you don't need to scramble. Solve it with your alg: a solve counts as a right answer. Keyboard turning uses your trainer keybinds.

The modes

  • Name: a random BH pattern. Click its picture or type its name.
  • Case: pick a subset (one COLL). You see one of its cases at the angle of its case picture, with only the BH stickers, and pick which case it is.
  • Angles: your standard angle is: T and U with the oriented corners in front; L with the oriented corner front-right and the other front corner's yellow facing you; Sune and Antisune with the oriented corner front-right; H and Pi with "chameleon eyes", the front corners' yellow facing left and right. H has two such angles. "Any angle" asks from all 4.
  • By pattern: use the 36 front-right patterns (12 patterns, each with the corner oriented or twisted either way) as presets. You get every ZBLL, at your standard angles or all 4, that shows a pattern you picked, and you recognise it from two sides. Solve it on the virtual cube or a smart cube, or use the scramble on a real cube, then show the answer.
  • Story: a whole ZBLL. Name the BH pattern at each corner, going round anticlockwise from the front-right corner (front-right, back-right, back-left, front-left). Look at each corner as if it were in front-right.
